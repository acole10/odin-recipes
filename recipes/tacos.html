Chicken Tacos

These savory chicken tacos are quick and easy.


Ingredients

  • 2-3 small chicken breasts.
  • Cilantro
  • Olive Oil
  • Kosher Salt
  • Cracked Black Pepper
  • Fresh Lime
  • White or Brown Rice
  • Bell Peppers
  • Shredded Cheese

Cooking Steps

  1. Cube each chicken breast into bite-sized pieces.
  2. Season chicken to your liking with Kosher salt and cracked black pepper.
  3. Boil water in a small pot and add rice - cook rice for 15min.
  4. Add olive oil to a medium pan and cook on medium heat until chicken is golden brown on the outside and cooked thoroughly.
  5. Shred cilantro and remove from its stalk - wash thoroughly after.
  6. Cut bell peppers into strips and wash thoroughly when finished.
  7. Put all ingredients into a tortilla, garnish with shredded cheese, and squeeze a fresh lime wedge on top.
  8. Enjoy!
