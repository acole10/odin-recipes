Honey Glazed Salmon Bites with Broccoli

Cubed salmon and broccoli

This perfectly cooked diced honey glazed salmon and broccoli is quick and easy.


Ingredients

  • 4oz Salmon fillet
  • Olive Oil
  • Kosher Salt
  • Cracked Black Pepper
  • Honey
  • Broccoli

Cooking Steps

  1. Remove salmon fillet from the fridge 30-45min prior to cooking.
  2. Pat dry with paper towels.
  3. Remove the skin from the salmon fillet.
  4. Cube the salmon fillet into bite size pieces.
  5. Season to your liking with Kosher salt, fresh cracked black pepper, and minced garlic.
  6. Preheat air fryer to 450 degrees.
  7. Boil a cup of water and place a steam basket in the pot of boiling water.
  8. Rinse broccoli.
  9. Place broccoli in steamer and let steam for 10min.
  10. Lightly coat the salmon bites in olive oil.
  11. Coat the salmon bites in honey until all pieces are evenly coated.
  12. Place in the air fryer for 10min.
  13. Remove from air fryer and let sit for 5min before serving.
