Charcoal Grilled Sirloin Steak

This perfectly grilled sirloin steak is quick and easy.


Ingredients

  • Sirloin Steak
  • 1 tsp minced garlic
  • Olive Oil
  • Salt
  • Pepper

Cooking Steps

  1. Allow steak to sit room temperature for 30 minutes
  2. Using a pea-size amount of olive oil, lather it on the steak
  3. Using salt, pepper, and garlic, season the steak to your liking
  4. Heat your grill and place the steaks on the grill - cooking each side for 5 minutes
  5. Remove the steak from the grill and let rest for 5 minutes before cutting
  6. Enjoy!
