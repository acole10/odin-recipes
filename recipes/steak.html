Blackstone Steak

Charcoal Grilled Sirloin Steak

This perfectly grilled sirloin steak is quick and easy.


Ingredients

  • 8-12oz Sirloin Steak
  • 1 tsp minced garlic
  • Avocado Oil
  • Kosher Salt
  • Cracked Black Pepper

Cooking Steps

  1. Remove from the fridge 30-45min prior to cooking.
  2. Pat dry with paper towels.
  3. Season to your liking with Kosher salt, fresh cracked black pepper, and minced garlic.
  4. Preheat one side of the Blackstone grill to 450-500 degrees.
  5. Lightly oil the blackstone with avocado oil.
  6. Cook each side of the steak for 4-5min before flipping.
  7. Remove steak and let rest for 5-10min before cutting
